# VizArc Brand Kit

**Tagline:** Architecture, Visualized.

## Brand personality
Modern · Precise · Scalable · Intelligent · Developer-first

## Color palette
| Token | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight Navy | `#0B1638` | Primary text, dark surfaces |
| Deep Background | `#06132F` | Dark-mode backgrounds |
| Royal Blue | `#165BFA` | Primary brand color, actions |
| Electric Cyan | `#00C8F2` | Accent, highlights |
| Cloud | `#F8FAFF` | Light-mode background |
| Ice Slate | `#E6F0FF` | Surfaces, dividers |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Dark-mode type, clean surfaces |

## Typography
- Primary: Plus Jakarta Sans (wordmark/UI headings)
- Supporting: Inter (body copy and utility labels)
- Fallback: Arial, sans-serif

## Logo variants
- `logos/vizarc-horizontal-light.*`: full lockup for light backgrounds
- `logos/vizarc-horizontal-dark.*`: full lockup for dark backgrounds
- `logos/vizarc-compact-*.*`: symbol + wordmark without tagline
- `logos/vizarc-symbol.*`: standalone mark
- `logos/vizarc-wordmark-*.*`: wordmark only

## Aspect-ratio guidance
- Horizontal logo: approximately 4.15:1; use in website headers and documentation.
- Compact logo: approximately 3.7:1; use in navigation bars and tight layouts.
- App icon/favicon: 1:1; use supplied sizes from 16px to 1024px.
- GitHub/social preview: 16:5 (1280×400); use for repository/social headers.
- Backgrounds: 16:9 for presentations/video and 4:5 for portrait/social layouts.

## Light and dark mode
Use the light variant on white/cloud/light surfaces. Use the dark variant on midnight/navy surfaces. The symbol's blue/cyan gradients are shared across both modes. Do not place the light wordmark on a white background or the dark wordmark on a navy background.

## Clear space and sizing
Keep clear space around the logo at least the height of the symbol's inner opening. Avoid shrinking the full lockup below 240px wide; use the standalone mark for smaller placements. Keep the tagline legible and do not stretch or skew the logo.

## Accessibility
Maintain strong contrast for the wordmark and tagline. For dark surfaces, use white type; for light surfaces, use midnight navy. Do not rely on cyan alone to communicate meaning.

## Files
SVG files are editable vector compositions. PNG files are raster exports at the dimensions indicated by their filenames.
